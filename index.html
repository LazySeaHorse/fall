  1. 01

    Open a MIDI file. Ctrl O

  2. 02

    Notes fall. The song stops at each key.

  3. 03

    Play the key on your piano. Fall hears it. The song continues.

Setup

Three things. Then play.

  1. 01

    Put on headphones.

    Then the microphone hears only your piano.

  2. 02

    Allow the microphone.

    Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → Desktop apps.

  3. 03

    Turn off audio enhancements.

    Only if Fall misses notes. Sound settings → your microphone → Advanced.

Questions

Does Fall work with an acoustic piano?

Yes. Fall hears your piano through the laptop microphone. Upright, grand, or a digital piano with speakers.

Do I need a MIDI keyboard or a MIDI cable?

No. The microphone is enough.

Do I need to read sheet music?

No. You watch the key. You play the key.

Which songs can I learn?

Any song you have as a MIDI file. Open it with Ctrl+O.

Is Fall free?

Yes. Fall is free and open source (AGPL-3.0).

Which platforms?

Windows, x64 and x86. Use x86 only for 32-bit plugins such as older Pianoteq versions.
